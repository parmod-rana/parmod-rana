# RULE.md — VARDHANI AI TEACHER–STUDENT AUTHORITY

## HARD RULE — DO NOT DEVIATE

This rule is authoritative for the VARDHANI HIGH-SPECIALIST R3/R6 market-brain project.

### 1. Teacher identity
**GPT-5.6 Sol (ChatGPT) is the expert teacher.**

The teacher must use its task-specific market knowledge, reasoning ability, and the user's agreed analysis framework to examine causal historical market states and teach the VARDHANI AI.

The teacher is responsible for expert interpretation of:
- 1m / 3m / 5m relationships and 15m context
- price action and candle structure
- trend, range, breakout, breakdown, failed break, reversal, exhaustion, continuation and transition
- multi-timeframe agreement and conflict
- volatility and India VIX
- NIFTY–SENSEX agreement/divergence
- genuine CE/PE, volume and OI information where physically available
- risk, MFE, MAE, target-before-stop, invalidation and uncertainty
- when PE, CE or WAIT/no-trade is the intelligent conclusion
- analogue/scenario reasoning and regime transitions

### 2. Student identity
**The VARDHANI specialist AI brain is the student.**

The student must learn the teacher's task-specific reasoning and knowledge from structured causal supervision plus genuine historical market experience.

The VARDHANI AI brain must never be relabelled as the teacher merely because it is later used to distill or compress another deployment model.

If a smaller deployment model is created later, call it a **deployment student / compressed descendant**, not the expert teacher.

### 3. Historical truth is not another teacher
Future market movement, realized returns, MFE/MAE, target-before-stop outcomes and other post-state results are **ground truth / reality feedback only**.

They may supervise whether the teacher/student reasoning was empirically correct, but they are not a second teacher and must not replace GPT-5.6 Sol as the expert-teaching authority.

### 4. No competing teacher authority
Do not introduce an empirical model, legacy model, indicator engine, R2 model, generic scorer, another LLM, or any other model as a co-teacher or superior teacher.

Auxiliary models may be used only as analysis instruments if they do not directly become teaching authority, override GPT expert supervision, or change the agreed hierarchy.

### 5. Causality
At historical timestamp `t`, teacher analysis may use only information genuinely available at or before `t`.

Future information must never leak into teacher inputs, expert labels, normalization, feature fitting, regime fitting, or action reasoning.

Future outcomes must remain physically/logically separate as ground-truth targets.

### 6. Frozen chronology
- 2022-01-01 through 2024-12-31: teacher-guided development/training universe.
- 2025: untouched read-only examination after the student is frozen. No fitting, optimizer gradients, normalization fitting, target-stat fitting, checkpoint selection or retuning.
- 2026: sealed for historical training; later prospective/live evidence only.

### 7. 15-minute authority rule
15m is context only.

1m / 3m / 5m retain lower-timeframe action authority.

15m may alter confidence, uncertainty or support WAIT, but it must not mechanically flip a genuine lower-timeframe CE thesis into PE or a genuine PE thesis into CE.

### 8. Genuine-data-only rule
Never fabricate missing D30, depth, spread, microprice, Greeks, SENSEX options, PCR, option OI/volume or any other unavailable historical field.

Missing information must use explicit presence masks or be declared unavailable.

### 9. Positive objective without falsifying evidence
The objective is to build a genuinely useful, winning specialist trading intelligence system.

Search positively for plausible sources of edge and useful intelligence, but never:
- lower evidence standards to force a winner
- cherry-pick periods
- alter holdouts
- fabricate evidence
- hide failure
- claim profitability from implementation accuracy alone

### 10. Paid GPU rule
Do not start a paid optimizer run unless:
- teacher supervision is frozen and audited
- the student architecture is frozen
- chronology/causality guards pass
- 2025 remains untouched
- code/data hashes are fixed
- the exact paid-training bundle is verified

### 11. Naming rule
Use these names consistently:

**TEACHER = GPT-5.6 Sol (ChatGPT)**  
**STUDENT = VARDHANI Master AI Brain**  
**GROUND TRUTH = actual historical future outcome**  
**DEPLOYMENT STUDENT = optional smaller compressed Android model**

Never call the VARDHANI Master AI the expert teacher.

### 12. Priority
If any later instruction, implementation detail, old file, old checkpoint, or legacy terminology conflicts with this RULE.md, this RULE.md wins unless the user explicitly changes the rule.
